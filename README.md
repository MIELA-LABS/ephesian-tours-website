# Ephesian Journeys

*An open door to the lands of the Bible.*

Small-group journeys through the Bible lands of Asia Minor for American church groups, personally escorted from Dallas–Fort Worth. Land arrangements are operated by Azim Tours (Kuşadası, Türkiye; TÜRSAB license no. 938).

**Status:** in development. Preview builds use sample itineraries and pricing.
